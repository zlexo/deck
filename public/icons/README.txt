Raster project/tool icons, served by the Deck gateway at /icons/<file>.

Referenced from a manifest's iconSvg as:
  <svg viewBox='0 0 32 32'>...<image href='/icons/name.png' .../></svg>

Flat namespace (no subdirectories). Allowed: .png .svg .webp .jpg
Square-ish sources render best; wide logos are letterboxed, never cropped.
